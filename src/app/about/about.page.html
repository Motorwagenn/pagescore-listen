About About PageScore Listen v0.1

A blind rating app for the listening rounds of PageScore, a bachelor's thesis on generating ambient music for reading.

Open a round, play each clip and mark whether it fits the mood and how good it sounds. Your ratings are listed under Results.

The app shows only clip numbers and moods, never which prompt made a clip, so the ratings stay unbiased.
